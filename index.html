$103 a Square Foot Separates 98125 Neighborhoods

79 sales, 13 median days on market and 45 active listings across 13 neighborhoods since Jul 20

By Adrian Willanger, Broker, Kelly Right

Since Jul 20, 98125 has closed 79 home sales at a median $496 per square foot, with homes selling in a median 13 days on market. Among neighborhoods with at least six sales, Olympic Hills led on price at $529 per square foot, while Pinehurst came in at $426. That gap is the first thing a seller should price against.

Speed varies even more than price. Meadowbrook and Victory Heights sold in a median 6 days. Cedar Park is the outlier, with sold homes taking a median 59 days against 13 for the ZIP.

Supply tells the next chapter. 98125 has 45 active listings and 11 pending, or about one pending for every 4 active homes. Lake City carries the busiest pipeline with 3 pending and 6 active.

What it means for you

If you are selling

Price to your neighborhood, not the ZIP. Olympic Hills and Pinehurst sit $103 per square foot apart, and buyers notice. Overpricing shows up as extra days on market.

If you are buying

Cedar Park is where patience still earns room to negotiate. In Victory Heights and Meadowbrook, homes sell in about a week, so come ready with a clean offer on day one.

Small lot homes

Nine sold since Jul 20 at a median $602 per square foot, against $496 on standard lots. They are smaller homes at lower prices, and they took longer to sell: a median 35 days, with 16 for sale and 1 pending now.
