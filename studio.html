Export checklist: recommended filters

Area

One ZIP per export (example: 98125). Extra ZIPs are detected and filtered out.

Statuses

Active, Pending, Pending Inspection or Contingent, Sold, Expired. Cancelled and withdrawn are ignored.

Date window

Sold: selling date in the last 120 days. Expired: last 90 days. Active and pending: current.

Property types

Residential, Townhouse, Condo. Leave out land, rentals, multifamily and manufactured.

Required columns

Status, Community, List Price, Sold Price, Listing Date, Selling Date, DOM, Square Footage.

Recommended columns

Original List Price (price cuts), Pending Date, Property Type, Zip Code, Address, Listing #, Beds, Baths, Year Built.

Sharing work with someone else

Each person runs the studio in their own browser, so several people can use it at once without seeing each other's data or drafts. There is no shared workspace: edits by one person do not appear for another.

To hand work off, click Save session file and send the file. The other person opens the studio, loads the same CSV export, then clicks Load session file. The session file holds the queue, content memory and report settings. It does not include the CSV, so send the export separately or have them pull the same one.

    Typo and data check

    A sold price under 75% or over 135% of its list price is almost always a typo. Fix it, leave it out, or keep it if the sale is real. Below the prices you will also see listings with no square footage, no neighborhood, a neighborhood that usually sits in another ZIP, or a different ZIP. Your choices are saved in the session file.

    Your details for these reports

    Your name and contact details go on the byline and contact block of every report you make: broadsheet, story PDF, carousel, cover and LinkedIn article. They stay in this browser tab and in your session file. Nothing is sent to a server.
